These additional permissions are granted by Launch Pad Foundation (the

("Shield"). They apply only to rights the Foundation can grant. They do not

under other licenses.

## 1. Organizer use

An organizer is a person or organization that produces, presents or genuinely
co-produces an event, including a venue or promoter. The Foundation expressly
permits organizers to use, copy, operate and modify the Software for their own
events and those of entities under common control, whether commercial or
nonprofit. This includes recurring events and multiple event brands. Charging
admission, public checkout, browser-code delivery, payment processing, ticket
delivery and recovery, and admission/door operations for those events are
permitted. Attendees and authorized event staff may access those functions.
An unrelated platform customer is not a co-producer merely because a service
agreement uses that label.

## 2. Paid developer services and organizer control

Developers, contractors and agencies may charge for installing, customizing,
integrating, migrating, maintaining, securing and supporting organizer-controlled
installations, and for training their users. They may serve multiple organizers,
deliver modified source to them and act under each organizer's delegated
authority. A recurring maintenance fee or number of clients alone does not
make those services a prohibited platform.

An organizer-controlled installation is a separate deployment for which the
organizer retains ultimate authority over its event data, deployment and
payment-provider relationship, including the ability to replace its contractor
and continue operating without purchasing access to the contractor's ticketing
product. Ordinary cloud hosting and revocable technical administration by a
contractor are permitted. Account names, shared tools or templates, or separate
deployments alone do not establish or defeat organizer control.

## 3. Community source sharing

Original and modified Software may be shared for the activities permitted in
sections 1 and 2, including public code contributions, fixes and organizer
themes. Copies must preserve Shield, these additional permissions and the
applicable notices. Every recipient receives these permissions directly from
the Foundation; recipients need not retain the same developer. This permission
does not extend to marketing, selling, licensing or operating a competing
ticketing-platform product or service for other organizers.

## 4. Platform products and services

These additional permissions do not permit providing a competing ticketing
product or service for independent organizers, including hosted, managed,
white-label or multi-tenant services and competing products distributed for
self-hosting. Those activities require a separate written commercial license
from the Foundation unless independently authorized by other applicable rights.
The Foundation welcomes negotiated commercial licensing inquiries.

The substance of the offering controls, not its name, nonprofit status, pricing
or deployment model. Charging nothing, using one deployment per customer,
calling a platform subscription "maintenance," or rebranding the Software
does not qualify a platform as permitted developer services. A ticketing
company performing only the services permitted by section 2 is not excluded
merely because it also operates a ticketing business.
