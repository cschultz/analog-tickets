# Analog Tickets licensing FAQ

This explains PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 with Analog Tickets Additional Permissions.
It does not replace the [license](../LICENSE.md) or
[additional permissions](../ADDITIONAL_PERMISSIONS.md).

## Can I use it for my festival or venue and sell tickets?

Yes. The permissions cover your own and genuinely co-produced events, recurring
events, multiple brands and commercial organizers. Public checkout and attendee
ticket access are permitted. Hosting and payment-processing costs are yours.
The TEST beta is not a statement of readiness for real admission sales.

## Can developers charge for installation and customization?

Yes, including integration, training and ongoing maintenance. A developer can
help many organizers. Each organizer must retain meaningful control of its
installation, data, deployment and payment-provider relationship and be able
to change developers without buying access to the developer's ticketing product.
Ordinary third-party hosting and delegated maintenance are allowed.

## When is a commercial platform license required?

When you provide a competing ticketing product or service for other independent
organizers: hosted, managed, white-label or sold/licensed for self-hosting.
A separate deployment per customer, a nonprofit provider or no platform fee
does not create an exception. The offering matters, not its billing label.
We welcome a conversation about a separately negotiated commercial agreement.

## Can I share fixes or change developers?

Yes. Community source sharing for permitted organizer and developer uses is
allowed with the legal documents and notices intact. Recipients receive their
own permissions directly from the Foundation. This is not permission to
distribute a competing ticketing-platform product.

## Must I publish my modifications or show a promotional badge?

These permissions impose neither requirement. Applicable legal notices still
remain, and you may not imply official endorsement or use excluded festival
assets without separate permission.

## Is this unrestricted open source? Do restrictions expire?

No. It is source-available and free for organizers, with negotiated commercial
platform licensing. There is no automatic timed conversion to MIT or Apache.
The line-of-business notice addresses Shield's discontinued-products provision.
Valid permissions for a released version are not withdrawn by a later policy
change, subject to the license's conditions and termination rules.

## What about the earlier Apache-licensed public repository?

Those earlier grants remain valid. The new beta does not retroactively restrict
the earlier work or applicable retained portions. Read
[prior-source licensing](PRIOR_SOURCE_LICENSES.md) and the preserved Apache
license. Third-party licenses also remain in force; the new grant covers only
rights the Foundation can grant. A source hash/path comparison is not a complete
legal provenance or exclusivity opinion.

## Who do I contact?

Launch Pad Foundation: use the [Get in Touch form](https://lp.foundation/about)
or the commercial contact in [NOTICE.txt](../NOTICE.txt). No hosting or guaranteed
support is included. For private vulnerability reporting follow
[SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md), not the public issue tracker or general inquiry
form. Do not send credentials or real customer data.
